# Swing Up early-access launch

Purpose: test qualified demand and pricing interest before adding more product scope.

## Live surfaces

- `/signup`: PostgreSQL-backed early-access request; consent required; free or proposed $19 USD/month pilot choice. No payment or product account is created.
- `/pricing`: visible price test with no checkout or implied reservation.
- `/signup/manage#<private-token>`: user-initiated deletion; only the hashed token is stored. Repeat signups never change an existing record or disclose its private token.
- `/research` and `/research/[id]`: saved original research snapshots and publication history. This is a record of observations, not certified investment performance.
- `/api/public/social-image/[id]`: immutable 1080 × 1350 PNG in the supplied Swing Up palette. Precise values are rendered directly, not generated by an image model.
- `/admin/growth`: owner-only 14-day aggregate report and prepared post copies. Uses the existing serious-signal read credential. No lead email export or publishing mutation is exposed.
- `/go/instagram`: profile-bio destination. Per-post `/go/[channel]/[id]` links preserve channel attribution through signup. Instagram captions include a non-clickable URL plus a bio instruction.

## Daily operation

An in-process Node scheduler on the existing always-running Railway application checks every minute. A PostgreSQL lease prevents concurrent app processes from generating the same slot. A unique signal schedule key and unique signal/channel delivery pair enforce idempotency.

The default cadence is 02:00, 10:00 and 16:00 UTC, every day (09:00, 17:00, 23:00 Asia/Bangkok). Each slot creates one research item and three platform copies: Facebook, Instagram, X. The morning slot prefers opportunity research; the second prefers sell research; the last prefers watch-out research. A company is not repeated within 24 hours. A preview pack is saved on first startup but never auto-posted.

The job reads the existing compact Swing Up valuation and price snapshots. It does not start scanners or request new committee runs. Price collection must be within 90 minutes; the foundation screen within 72 hours; prices, USD currency, model target/range and evidence must be present. The screen's classification must still agree with its price. Missing or stale data holds a slot. There is no guarantee that every slot will qualify.

Collection time is not exchange trade time. The latest recorded value can be a last close during closed markets. Valuation screens have no verified news event or target horizon: both remain explicitly unestablished. Confidence is an uncalibrated model score, never a probability of profit. Existing Serious Signal eligibility and committee authority remain false.

Provider acceptance is recorded separately from confirmed publication. An interrupted publishing call is reconciled against its unique research URL; it is never blindly retried. Old missed slots are held, not posted in a burst after a restart. Read-only owner status reports failures.

## One-time publishing connection

Configure on the existing Railway app service only:

- `SWING_UP_GROWTH_ENABLED=true`
- `SWING_UP_SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_ENABLED=true`
- `BUFFER_ACCESS_TOKEN`
- `BUFFER_ORGANIZATION_ID`
- `BUFFER_FACEBOOK_CHANNEL_ID`
- `BUFFER_INSTAGRAM_CHANNEL_ID`
- `BUFFER_X_CHANNEL_ID`
- `APP_BASE_URL`: the public HTTPS origin used for image fetches and links

The runtime also requires the existing production environment and canonical app service ID. It is inactive in local builds, preview services and other scanner workers. Setting `SWING_UP_SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_ENABLED=false` stops new external publications without stopping signup or research generation. Previously accepted provider posts must be managed at the provider.

Connect the owner's Swing Up Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and X account in Buffer. The runtime verifies organization/channel membership, matching platform, and a Swing Up account name before publishing. Put the public `/go/instagram` URL in Instagram's bio. Account login/authorization is a one-time owner step; no social credentials are collected by Swing Up.

Buffer uses its current GraphQL API at `https://api.buffer.com`. Reference: https://developers.buffer.com/reference.html . Images are public HTTPS URLs. The first external post still needs end-to-end confirmation after real accounts are connected. No provider charge or paid plan is automatically purchased by this implementation.

## Interpreting demand

The owner report separates browser-session visits, unique unverified email submissions, free requests, $19 pilot interest and actual payments (zero until billing exists). Bots, shared networks, returning visitors and unverified addresses can affect counts. A pilot-interest checkbox is not proof of payment willingness.

Review after 14 days of actual distribution. Identify the channel producing relevant signups, interview interested users, offer a concrete paid pilot with clear features and delivery terms, and measure payment plus repeat use before investing more. Do not treat a fixed signup count or 1,000 fans as a guaranteed path to one million users.

## Data and operations

Rate-limit keys are HMAC hashes of a network identifier and time bucket. Raw addresses are not stored in the lead table. Cleanup removes expired request limits, visits after 90 days and leads after 365 days. Self-service deletion is immediate for the live lead record; backups and platform logs have separate retention. Email verification, automatic welcome email, checkout, ad buying, cold outreach and automatic comment replies are not enabled.

The migration only adds growth tables. Existing scanner data, product users and signal authority are untouched. Railway's existing `prisma migrate deploy` startup applies it. Do not accept unrelated staged Railway changes.

Verification: TypeScript, lint, production build, financial-data/copy checks, and a real PostgreSQL CI test covering concurrent signup, consent persistence, deletion, rate limiting, attribution deduplication, owner authorization, scheduler leases and ambiguous publishing recovery. Provider calls in integration tests are simulated; database calls are real.
