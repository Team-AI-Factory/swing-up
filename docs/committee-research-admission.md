# Committee research admission

PR262 event jobs now distinguish research eligibility from alert publication. The research shortlist accepts a score of 55 (instead of requiring the publication gate's 72), exact issuer mapping of at least 95, materiality of at least 45, a dated event within 72 hours, and at least 200 characters of attributed source text. Existing fully qualified candidates retain their route. These scores are internal priorities, not probabilities of a profitable trade.

Missing current prices, unavailable halt checks, unclear direction in recognized material events, missing company-relative magnitude, one-source evidence and partially read filings can reach the 14-role Committee. Gaps and research status accompany every role's input. Direction stays `unknown` until evidenced; it is never defaulted to upside. Generic noise, ambiguous issuers, unsupported sector associations, unsafe source URLs and headline-only near misses remain excluded. Partial sources retain the existing 48-hour unread-source expiry.

The existing shared provider budgets, $0.75 review reservation, 20-review rolling cap, $10 AI daily fuse, schedules and notification configuration are unchanged. Ready candidates are preferred within an event's shortlist. Research reviews remain internal; unresolved required evidence, unavailable fresh market/halt checks, unknown direction or incomplete sources cannot create a Serious Signal. All 14 roles and existing Final Judge/publication authority remain required. A completed needs-more-data review stays in the durable retry queue; a substantive rejection is not endlessly rereviewed. Existing same-evidence and paid-review cooldowns apply.

Financial enrichment now compares all supported accounting aliases, chooses current periods and latest amendments, excludes future or invalid dates, checks the returned issuer identity when supplied, adds supported IFRS facts, and distinguishes annual revenue from quarterly figures. Verified contract/financing/fine amounts are scaled against an eligible annual-revenue or shares denominator and the magnitude gate is reassessed. This fixes the circular requirement to prove company-relative impact before retrieving company fundamentals.

Validation covers research admission with missing/stale prices, unavailable halt state, partial evidence, low-score near misses and unknown direction; unchanged spending reservations, duplicate suppression and publication guards; the actual 14-role orchestrator with mocked provider responses; complete-needs-data retry behavior; and financial aliases, dates, identity and company-scale calculations. Test fixtures are not live signal proof.

Remaining quality work: broaden direct issuer/regulator coverage, improve independent source fallback, cache verified financial facts durably with field-level dates, distinguish expected market-closed prices from delayed in-session quotes, and report required-field completeness and event-to-review latency separately from connectivity. No claim of a sustained sub-5% source failure rate or guaranteed alert frequency is made by this change.
